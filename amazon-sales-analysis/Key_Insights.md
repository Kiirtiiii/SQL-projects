# Key Insights

This document summarizes the main findings from the SQL analysis of the Amazon Sales dataset.

## Revenue

- The dataset generated a total revenue of ₹243,845.
- Electronics was the highest revenue-generating category.
- Home Appliances was the second-highest revenue-generating category.

## Products

- Refrigerator generated the highest revenue.
- Laptop and Smartphone were among the top revenue-generating products.
- Smartwatch recorded the highest number of units sold.

## Payments

- PayPal was the most frequently used payment method.
- Revenue was distributed across multiple payment methods, with PayPal contributing the most.

## Orders

- Completed orders were the most common order status.
- Pending and Cancelled orders accounted for a smaller share of total orders.

## Locations

- Miami generated the highest revenue among all locations.
- Revenue varied across different cities, showing differences in regional sales performance.

## Conclusion

This analysis highlights product performance, customer purchasing patterns, payment preferences, and revenue trends using SQL queries. It also demonstrates the use of SQL concepts such as filtering, grouping, aggregation, sorting, and business-oriented analysis.
